DUAL-CHECK RAIL

Why Simple Passwords Fail ATO Defense

If an attacker steals a player's credentials via phishing, session hijacking, or SIM swapping, simple 2FA can fail.

Ninja-Bet's dual-check payout rail enforces that money can only exit to a bank account whose Bank Verification Number (BVN) matches the verified national identity on file. Even if an attacker controls the account, they cannot steal the funds.
